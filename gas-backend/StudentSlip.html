2 Medical Insurance Coverage Gate (CA Ed Code 35330)

WVUSD Mandatory Insurance Policy Notice:

Under California Education Code Section 35330 and WVUSD Board Policy, all students participating in school-sponsored field trips and excursions must have medical and accidental injury insurance coverage.

Because your student is currently uninsured, this permission slip cannot be submitted until medical insurance is obtained. Please contact your school's health office immediately to enroll in low-cost district student accident insurance.
